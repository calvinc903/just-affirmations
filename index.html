Just Affirmations?

Your words. Your widgets. Your reminders.

A simple place for affirmations you want to come back to. Write your own words, keep them in groups, and choose where you want to see them.

Make it yours

  • Create and reorder affirmations in named groups.
  • Give each widget set its own group, color and rotation interval.
  • Add Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.
  • Choose optional daily reminders or intervals within waking hours.
  • Read the full current and next affirmation in Rotation details.

For iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Your library stays on your device and is shared with the app's widgets. No account, ads, analytics or purchases.

A few things to know

iOS controls when widgets visibly refresh. Reminders queue up to 60 upcoming occurrences across your groups, so open the app before the next unqueued time and after changing time zones. Focus and notification settings affect delivery.
